### 📚 Tech Philosophy

**Agility over Agile** ([Allen Holub](https://x.com/allenholub/status/1264247513202094080)). Agility is the property the [Agile Manifesto](https://agilemanifesto.org/) actually names. The methodology industry that grew around capital-A Agile has often drifted from those principles.

- **Co-signatory** of [Minimum CD](https://minimumcd.org) and **certified** by Dave Farley on the Continuous Delivery discipline.
- **Continuous Delivery** ([Dave Farley](https://www.davefarley.net/), [Jez Humble](https://continuousdelivery.com/)) operationalizes the first Manifesto principle: the early and continuous delivery of valuable software.
- **Adaptive agile project management** ([Frédéric Leguédois](https://www.leguedois.fr/)): fix the cost and the quality, let the scope adapt under client arbitrage.
- **Lean thinking, DevOps**: working software over comprehensive documentation. Production validation over orchestrated demos.
- **Patron of**: [Modern Software Engineering](https://www.youtube.com/@ModernSoftwareEngineeringYT) YouTube channel.
